## Why ADRs matter here

Six months from now the reasoning behind a decision will be gone, and only the
decision will remain. An ADR is cheap insurance: half a page recording what was
true at the time, what was considered, and what was given up.

The rule: **if a decision would be expensive to reverse, or if someone might
reasonably wonder "why on earth did they do that", write an ADR.**
