## Inclusion standard

Only primary and authoritative material: official documentation, university
courses, books by practitioners, peer-reviewed papers, and engineering writing
from the teams that built the system.

**Excluded:** listicles, "X in 5 minutes", AI-generated tutorials, and blog
posts that restate documentation. A blog post earns a place only when it is the
best available explanation of something (Kleppmann on distributed locking) or
when it is the primary source (an engineering team describing its own system).

Every entry states *why it is here*. An unannotated link is a bookmark, not a
resource.
