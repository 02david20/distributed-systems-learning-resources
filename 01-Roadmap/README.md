# Roadmap

Three documents drive the learning plan:

| Document | Purpose | How often it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Six-Month Roadmap](6-month-roadmap.md) | The 24-week plan | Rarely — only when scope genuinely changes |
| [Current Week](current-week.md) | What I am doing right now | Every week |
| [Learning Principles](learning-principles.md) | How I study, and why | Rarely |

The roadmap assumes **8–10 hours per week**. That is roughly:

- 3–4 hours reading and note-taking
- 3–4 hours hands-on labs
- 1–2 hours review, writing up, and answering my own open questions

If a week slips, the correct response is to move the whole plan back by a
week rather than to skip the labs. The labs are where the learning happens.
